What's inside

Built for the rhythm of a school day

Three tools that cover the whole loop — marking attendance, catching problems early, and turning the numbers into something useful.

Check-in

Tap, scan, or self check-in

Mark a whole class present with one tap, let students scan a QR code as they walk in, or auto-detect attendance from the seating chart.

Alerts

Catch patterns before they become problems

Roster flags three missed days in a row, unusual tardiness, or a sudden change — and notifies the right person automatically.

Reports

Reports admins actually open

Attendance by class, grade, or student, exported the way your district needs it — no more stitching spreadsheets together by hand.

How it works

From bell to record, in three steps

01

Open today's register

Every class roster loads automatically at the start of the period, pulled straight from your school's schedule.

02

Mark the room

Tap through present, late, and absent — or let students self check-in. Changes save instantly, no submit button required.

03

Let Roster do the follow-up

Parents get notified of an absence, admins see it on their dashboard, and the record is ready for reporting — all without extra work.
